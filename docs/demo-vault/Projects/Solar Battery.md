# Solar Battery

Goal: store the midday surplus of the solar panels in a home **battery** and use it in the evening.

## Options
- 10 kWh battery with hybrid inverter
- Smaller 5 kWh battery, extendable later
- Wait for the next generation of sodium cells

## Notes from the installer
> [!note] Sizing
> A battery larger than the evening consumption rarely pays off.

The battery management should keep the charge between 15 % and 90 % in winter.
Compare with the [[Heat Pump]] consumption before deciding.
